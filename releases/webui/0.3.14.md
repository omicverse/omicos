## 中文

- 新增「泛AI4S」领域：在领域切换器里选择，收录量子计算、天文、地球科学、计算化学、数学建模、科学机器学习等方向的智能体和技能。
- 10 月 31 日（北京时间）前，所有账号的泛AI4S 都可免费使用 Pro。
- 新增「教程」页：在左侧「…」菜单里打开，分「文本教程」和「视频教程」两栏。文本教程直接在 OmicOS 里阅读使用文档，界面语言是中文时显示中文文档；视频教程汇集了 OmicOS 的介绍视频，点击后在浏览器里观看。
- 在桌面端阅读文本教程时，文档里指向其他网站的链接会在系统浏览器中打开。
- 修复：输入框靠近窗口顶部或窗口较小时，「+」菜单会根据上下空间选择展开方向，不再被裁掉一部分；放不下时可以滚动。

## English

- New General AI4S domain in the domain switcher, with agents and skills for quantum computing, astronomy, earth science, computational chemistry, mathematical modelling, scientific machine learning and more.
- Until October 31 (Beijing time), every account can use General AI4S Pro for free.
- New Tutorials page, opened from the "…" menu in the left rail, with two tabs. Text tutorials show the user guide right inside OmicOS, in Chinese when the interface is in Chinese. Video tutorials collect OmicOS introduction videos, which open in your browser.
- In the desktop app, links in the text tutorials that point to other sites now open in your system browser.
- Fixed: when the message box is near the top of the window or the window is small, the "+" menu now opens in whichever direction has room instead of being cut off, and scrolls when it still does not fit.
