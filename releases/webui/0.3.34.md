## 中文

- Bot：顶部右侧的侧栏按钮在 Bot 页里打开 Bot 或群的详情；Bot 的工作方向以小字显示在名字旁边。
- Bot 可以用希腊、北欧神话里的神作头像和名字；群里成员要批准的操作可以先交给群管理员判断，拿不准再问你。此项需配合新版 Core。
- 模型菜单里的名称更简洁；模型设置的搜索框始终可用，下拉框改为主题弹层。
- Antigravity 账户可以按当前位置列出可用模型和共享额度（需配合新版 Core）；打开较早阅读位置的对话时，恢复不再中断。

## English

- Bots: the right-panel button at the top opens the bot's or group's details in the Bot view, and each bot's work direction shows in small text beside its name.
- Bots can take a Greek or Norse god's portrait and name, and a group admin can review members' approval requests first, asking you only when unsure. Requires the matching Core update.
- Cleaner model names in pickers; model settings keep their search box and use themed dropdowns.
- Antigravity accounts list their available models and shared quotas for the current location (with the matching Core); reopening a conversation at an earlier reading position no longer stalls.
