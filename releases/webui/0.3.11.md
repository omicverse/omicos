## 中文

- 首页生成推荐时显示分步进度条：能看到正在进行哪一步、已用多长时间；生成失败时会标出失败的步骤和原因，编辑精选可直接重试，失败不扣次数。
- 编辑精选的刷新按钮旁新增「贴近 ↔ 新颖」滑杆：往左更贴近你的研究，往右探索更多相邻方向。调整后在下次刷新时生效，拖动本身不扣次数。
- 修复：发送新消息时，上一轮建议的任务卡片不再跳到正在生成的新回答里。

## English

- Homepage recommendations show a step-by-step progress bar while they are generated, with the current step and elapsed time. A failed run marks the step that failed and why; editorial picks can be retried right away, and failures do not use a refresh.
- Editorial picks add a Close ↔ Explore slider beside the refresh button. Left stays closer to your research, right explores more adjacent directions. It applies on the next refresh, and moving it costs nothing.
- Fixed: suggested-task cards from an earlier turn no longer jump into a new answer while it is being written.
