## 中文

- 云算力会根据任务的 CPU、内存、临时磁盘、运行时长和预算推荐可运行的规格；Cell Ranger 等大任务会计入临时磁盘需求，提交仍需你确认。
- 可统一查看 Codex、DeepSeek、Kimi 和 OpenRouter 的订阅额度、API 余额及 Key 预算，并手动兑换 Codex 额度重置卡。
- “OmicOS 云模型”卡片会正确替换旧的后台目录条目；模型列表以服务端配置为准。
- 暂停的对话上传会自动恢复，切换账号或删除对话后会清理对应的重试。

## English

- Cloud compute recommends a runnable profile from a task's CPU, memory, scratch disk, duration, and budget requirements. Large jobs such as Cell Ranger include scratch needs, and submission still requires your approval.
- View subscription quotas, API balances, and key budgets for Codex, DeepSeek, Kimi, and OpenRouter in one place, and redeem Codex quota reset cards manually.
- The OmicOS cloud model card correctly replaces the old catalog entry, with models supplied by the server.
- Paused conversation uploads resume automatically; switching accounts or deleting a conversation clears its pending retries.
